## Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · AlwaysReady · Parker Digital & Print Services

### 1. Who we are

AlwaysReady is a brand of Parker Digital & Print Services, based at 82A James Carter Road, Mildenhall, IP28 7DE. We provide CQC inspection readiness tools for adult social care managers in the UK.

**Data Controller:** Parker Digital & Print Services
**Contact:** support@alwaysready.uk

Where our platform stores and processes personal data on behalf of your organisation, Parker Digital & Print Services also acts as a **data processor**. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) governing this relationship is available on request.

### 2. Part A — Marketing website (alwaysready.uk)

#### What we collect and why

We collect name, work email, organisation name, CQC Location ID, service type and marketing consent when you:

- Start a free trial via our website
- Subscribe to our blog or newsletter
- Make an enquiry via our Contact form
- Join our waitlist

**Legal basis:** Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR) for delivering resources and trial access; Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for marketing communications.

#### How we store it

Form data submitted via our website is sent directly to the AlwaysReady platform API and stored as a contact or lead record in our Supabase database.

### 3. Part B — AlwaysReady Platform (portal.alwaysready.uk)

This section applies to organisations and individuals who use the AlwaysReady inspection readiness platform.

#### What we collect and why

When your organisation uses the platform, we collect and store the following categories of data:

**Account and identity data**
- Organisation name and CQC Location ID
- CQC rating and inspection history (fetched automatically from the public CQC API at signup)
- Admin account: full name, email address, role
- Team member accounts: full name, username (email address), role
- Visitor accounts: full name, email address, access expiry date

**Compliance and operational data**
- KLOE framework responses, evidence notes, ratings and action items entered by your team
- Documents and files uploaded as evidence
- Sub-service configurations (e.g. dementia care, end of life care)
- Audit trail entries recording changes to compliance records

**HR and workforce data**
- Staff profiles: name, job title, contract type
- Training records and certifications
- Holiday and absence records

**Support and communication data**
- Support tickets submitted through the platform or by email
- Replies and correspondence relating to support requests

**Legal acceptance data**
- Timestamp and version reference of the Terms & Conditions accepted at trial signup

**Billing data**
- Subscription status and billing history (managed by Stripe; we do not store card details)

**Legal basis:** Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR) — processing is necessary to deliver the platform services your organisation has subscribed to.

#### How we use this data

We use the data above solely to:

- Provide, maintain and improve the AlwaysReady platform
- Enable your team to track and manage CQC compliance
- Send transactional communications (account confirmations, support replies, subscription notifications)
- Respond to support requests
- Generate AI-assisted newsletter draft text when requested by the organisation admin (see below)

We do not use platform data for marketing purposes. We never sell your data.

#### AI newsletter drafting tool

The platform includes an AI-assisted newsletter drafting tool available to organisation admins. When used, the content of your newsletter prompt (topics, themes and any text you provide) is processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate a draft. We do not send newsletters on your behalf — the draft is for you to copy, edit and send through your own preferred channel.

**Important:** do not enter personal details of residents, patients, families or individual staff members into the newsletter tool. The tool is for generating general compliance communications only.

The Anthropic API is used solely to process newsletter drafting requests and is not used to train AI models on your data. See anthropic.com/privacy for Anthropic's privacy policy.

#### Data you enter on behalf of others

Your organisation may enter personal data relating to your staff members (names, training records, HR information). Your organisation is the **data controller** for this data. AlwaysReady processes it solely on your instructions as a **data processor**. A Data Processing Agreement is available on request.

|---|---|

You can download a full export of your organisation's compliance data and evidence files at any time from within the platform (Account → Billing → Export your data). This export is available throughout your subscription and during the 30-day post-cancellation period.

Click the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or email hello@alwaysready.uk. We will act within 5 working days. Unsubscribing from marketing does not affect transactional emails related to your account or subscription.

### 8. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Restrict** how we process your data
- **Port** your data to another service
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any right, contact hello@alwaysready.uk. We will respond within one calendar month.

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

## Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

AlwaysReady subscriptions are Pay As You Go with no lock-in and no cancellation fees.

### How subscriptions work

Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance through Stripe. You pay at the start of each billing period and access continues until the end of that period.

### How to cancel

Cancel at any time directly from within the platform: **Account → Billing → Cancel my subscription**. This opens the Stripe Customer Portal where you can cancel immediately. You can also email hello@alwaysready.uk and we will confirm your cancellation within 2 working days.

### What happens after you cancel

| Scenario | What happens |
|---|---|
| Cancel before next billing date | No further charge. Access continues until the end of your current billing period. |
| Billing period ends | Platform access is deactivated. Your data is retained for 30 days. |
| Within 30 days of deactivation | You can reactivate your subscription and regain full access, or download your data via the export function. |
| After 30 days | All organisation data is permanently deleted from our systems. |

### Exporting your data

We strongly recommend downloading your data before cancelling. You can export all your compliance records and evidence files at any time from **Account → Billing → Export your data**. Two exports are available: one for all your records and data as CSV files, and one for all uploaded evidence files as a ZIP archive.

### Refunds

We do not offer pro-rata refunds for partial months. If you believe a payment has been taken in error, contact hello@alwaysready.uk and we will review it on a case-by-case basis.

### Free trial

The 14-day free trial requires no payment details and ends automatically. If you do not activate a subscription at the end of your trial, your account will be deactivated and your data will be retained for 30 days before permanent deletion.
